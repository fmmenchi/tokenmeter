# Licences

What tokenmeter contains that someone else made, and what it carries that was made for it.

## tokenmeter

MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 fmmenchi. The text is in the repository, in `LICENSE`.

## Sparkle

The app updates itself with [Sparkle](https://sparkle-project.org) ([ADR 0011](../adr/0011-updates.md)), which is built into the
app. Sparkle is under a permissive MIT-style licence with the copyright notices of its authors and of the components it
bundles; its text is kept in the repository, in `Licenses/Sparkle-LICENSE.txt`. Whether the app bundle itself must carry that
text too is not verified.

## The skins

The colours of the [skins](./skins.md) are those of four editor themes, all under the MIT licence. Only numbers are taken, a few
colours of each, and no code or artwork. The notices are kept as the themes wrote them, in the repository, in
`Licenses/Skins-LICENSES.txt`.

| Skin       | Theme                       | Licence and copyright                                                  |
| :--------- | :-------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Dracula    | Dracula                     | MIT, Copyright (c) 2023 Dracula Theme                                  |
| One Dark   | One Dark Pro, and One Light | MIT, Copyright (c) 2013-2022 Binaryify; Copyright (c) 2016 GitHub Inc. |
| Catppuccin | Catppuccin, Mocha and Latte | MIT, Copyright (c) 2021 Catppuccin                                     |
| Nord       | Nord                        | MIT, Copyright (c) 2016-present Sven Greb                              |

Three things are derived by us and are not the theme's: the light version of Dracula, whose own light theme is not in its free
repository; the card and the track of One Dark's light version, taken from the One Light background; and nine quota colours that
were under 3 : 1 against what the bar is drawn on, each darkened or lightened with its hue kept until it reached 3 : 1: Dracula
dark's red (`FF5555` to `FF5F5F`), One Dark dark's red (`E05561` to `E46B75`), One Light's blue, orange and red (`4078F2`,
`C18401`, `E45649` to `3973F2`, `A57101`, `E03F31`), Catppuccin Latte's teal, peach and red (`179299`, `FE640B`, `D20F39` to
`127278`, `B44201`, `CD0F38`) and Nord Snow Storm's orange (`D08770` to `C56C4F`). The names are the
themes' names, used to say where the colours come from; tokenmeter is not made, approved or endorsed by any of them. This is
what the author did to respect the licences, not legal advice.

## The easter eggs

The 36 images of the [seasonal easter eggs](./seasonal-easter-eggs.md) were **generated by the author with an AI image tool**
and were not taken from anyone's artwork. They are used as they were generated, with the background removed and the size
reduced. Images made by an AI may not be covered by copyright.

## The icons of the app

The app icon, the menu-bar gauge and the symbols of the panel's buttons are the author's own drawings and Apple's system
symbols.
