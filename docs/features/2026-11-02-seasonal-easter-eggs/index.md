# Seasonal easter eggs

For a week around Halloween, Christmas and Easter, the panel carries two small details: an icon of the event on the bars,
and a corner piece on the cards. A bit of humour, and nothing that changes what the panel says.

|        |            |
| :----- | :--------- |
| Date   | 2026-11-02 |
| Status | in design  |

## Intent

An app that sits in the menu bar all day can afford to smile now and then. The details are small, they come only in the
week of an event, they are always the same two places, and they never touch the colours or the figures: the theme the
person chose, and the meaning of a bar, stay as they are.

They are not optional: there is no switch. That is the author's decision, taken because a bit of humour is good for a day;
the proposal says what keeps them harmless.

The colours of the theme are a separate feature, the skins, and come after this one.
