# Unused week, proposal

The design that was chosen, variant B of three. It was drawn as a canvas in Claude, deleted once the feature was implemented.

| Variant | What it was                                                                                  |
| :------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A       | A notice only, with a fourth switch in Settings › Notifications.                             |
| **B**   | **The notice, and a line under the Weekly bar in the panel that shows whatever the switch.** |
| C       | The panel only: a chip "unused" beside the Weekly figure, no notice.                         |

## What changes

| Surface                  | What it shows                                                                                                             |
| :----------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Settings › Notifications | A fourth switch, **Plenty left near the reset**: "The weekly has 50% or more left with under 2 days to go." Off at first. |
| The notification         | `Claude · Weekly`, then `58% left · resets in 1d 20h · most of the week is still unused`.                                 |
| The panel                | Under the Weekly bar's detail line, in the accent colour: `Most of the week is still unused`.                             |
| Unchanged                | The three notices of before, the pace mark, the widget.                                                                   |
