tokenmeter 0.11.0

Released on 2026-11-09.

  • The stats draw every window as a strip of blocks, with time running from left to right: the 5 hours over the last 14 days, a column for each day with a block for each window, and the weekly as one block for each week of the range, filled as a bar is. A dashed block is a window the app did not see. Point at a block for its date and its figure, in the reading chosen with Show on the bars (Stats).
  • The 5-hour windows count in the verdict. A window shorter than a day looks at the last 14 days and needs 12 of them before it counts.
  • The verdict can say Move up: the ceiling was reached at least twice and in at least 15% of the windows. It says the plan is tight; it does not say which plan to take.
  • A notice says when the weekly limit is not known yet, and that the verdict is then drawn from the 5 hours alone.
  • The welcome's pages on the stats and on the suggestion describe the strips and the four words (The welcome).
